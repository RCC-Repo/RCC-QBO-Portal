Last updated: November 23, 2026

The QBO Coding Portal ("the Portal") is an internal software tool owned and operated by Redemption City Church ("the Church"). It helps the Church's finance staff review and correct how transactions are coded in the Church's own QuickBooks Online company.

1. Who may use it. The Portal is licensed only to the Church and to staff or volunteers the Church authorizes to manage its finances. It isn't offered, sold or licensed to anyone else.

2. What it does. With permission granted through Intuit's sign-in, the Portal reads the Church's QuickBooks Online accounting data and, when an authorized user chooses to save, updates the account and department (location) on existing transactions. It may also create journal entries that an authorized user reviews and approves. It doesn't access payment, payroll or banking credentials.

3. Your responsibilities. Authorized users are responsible for reviewing changes before saving them and for keeping access to the computer running the Portal secure.

4. Disconnecting. The Church can disconnect the Portal at any time from within the Portal, or in QuickBooks Online under Settings › Apps (Connected apps). Disconnecting stops all access.
