QBO Coding Portal: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

The QBO Coding Portal ("the Portal") is an internal tool used only by Redemption City Church ("the Church") to review the coding of its own QuickBooks Online transactions. This policy explains what data the Portal handles and how.

What data the Portal accesses

  • The Church's QuickBooks Online accounting data: the chart of accounts, departments (locations), classes, and transactions with their dates, amounts, payees, memos and coding.
  • Files the Church's staff choose to upload: bank statement downloads, QuickBooks report exports, and giving-platform reports.

Donor information

When a giving report is uploaded, donor names, email addresses, mailing addresses and free-text notes are removed before the file is saved. Donor ID numbers are replaced with a one-way code. The Portal uses giving reports only to total gifts by fund and to match deposits; it doesn't store donor identities.

Where data is kept

  • The Portal runs on a computer controlled by the Church. Data stays on that computer and is exchanged only with Intuit's QuickBooks Online service.
  • The sign-in tokens Intuit issues are stored encrypted on that computer, with the encryption key held in the operating system's secure keychain.
  • The Portal keeps a log of the changes it makes (date, transaction, previous and new values) so they can be reviewed.

Sharing

The Portal doesn't sell, rent or share data with anyone. It doesn't send data to advertisers or analytics services, and it doesn't use cookies for tracking.

Access and removal

Only staff or volunteers authorized by the Church use the Portal. The Church can disconnect the Portal from QuickBooks Online at any time, which deletes its stored sign-in. The Church can also delete uploaded files and logs from the computer running the Portal.

Security

Access to the Portal is limited to the computer it runs on, unless the Church deliberately configures otherwise and sets a password. The Portal rejects requests that don't come from its own pages.

Changes

The Church may update this policy. The current version will always be at this page.
